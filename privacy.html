Privacy policy

Channels Studio is a private tool that runs only on its owner's own computer. Last updated: November 2026.

What it accesses

When the owner signs in with Google, Channels Studio asks for read-only access to YouTube data and YouTube Analytics for the signed-in channel. It reads video statistics such as views, likes, comments, impressions, click-through rate and watch time.

How the data is used

The data is shown to the owner inside Channels Studio on their own computer, to compare how their videos perform. It is not used for anything else.

Where the data is kept

Everything, including the sign-in tokens, is stored only in files on the owner's computer. Nothing is sent to any server other than Google's own APIs. No data is sold, shared, or given to anyone.

Removing access

Access can be removed at any time in the Google Account under Security › Third-party apps with account access, or with the Disconnect button in Channels Studio. Deleting the Channels Studio data folder removes all stored data.

Channels Studio's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
